Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PathBloom: Life Simulator ("we," "our," or "us") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users. All game data (saves, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage and is never sent to any server.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics. The app does not use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics services.

Data Storage

All game progress is saved exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers. You can clear all saved data at any time through your device's app settings or browser storage management.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. As no data is collected at all, children can use the app safely without any privacy concerns.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated date will be reflected above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions, use the developer contact listed on PathBloom's official store page.
